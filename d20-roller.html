How to roll a d20

Open the roller, choose d20, then press Roll Dice or click/tap the tray. Tap d20 more than once if you want multiple d20s in the same pool. The result and individual dice are recorded in History.

ADVANTAGE & DISADVANTAGE

One-click ADV and DIS

The roller includes dedicated ROLL ADV and ROLL DIS controls. Advantage rolls two d20s and keeps the higher result; disadvantage rolls two d20s and keeps the lower result. They are quick actions, so you do not have to rebuild your normal dice pool.

AT THE TABLE

When a d20 is useful

Use a d20 whenever your game asks for a twenty-sided roll: attacks, ability checks, saving throws, initiative, contested checks, or any homebrew rule that calls for one. The roller does not hide character-specific arithmetic inside the die result, so the numerical roll stays easy to verify.

Check your chance before you roll
