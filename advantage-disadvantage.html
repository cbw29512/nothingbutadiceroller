ADVANTAGE / DISADVANTAGE

D20 advantage and disadvantage roller

Advantage and disadvantage change a d20 roll without adding another arithmetic modifier. Roll two d20s, then keep the higher result for advantage or the lower result for disadvantage.

HOW IT WORKS

Advantage keeps the higher d20

With advantage, roll two d20s and use the higher numerical result. Nothing But A Dice Roller exposes this as ROLL ADV on desktop and ADV on mobile so it takes one action.

HOW IT WORKS

Disadvantage keeps the lower d20

With disadvantage, roll two d20s and use the lower result. The dedicated ROLL DIS or DIS control performs that comparison automatically and then resets, so it does not silently affect the next roll.

PROBABILITY

Why advantage changes your odds

Keeping the better of two d20s shifts the chance of meeting many target numbers upward, while keeping the worse shifts it downward. The size of that change depends on the target and any modifier you add after the die result.

TRANSPARENCY

The dice still stay visible

The app keeps the operation easy to inspect: two physical-looking d20s are rolled and the kept result is reported. That makes it suitable for a shared table where players want to see what happened rather than trust a hidden comparison.
