PRIVACY

What the app stores and why.

This page describes the current production design in plain language. Last updated August 22, 2026.

GUEST MODE

Browser-local data

You can use the standard roller without creating an account. The app may store preferences and working data in your browser so the experience survives a reload. This can include sound/Keep Dice preferences, roll history, selected visual preferences, and browser-saved custom dice sets.

Browser-local data stays with that browser profile unless you explicitly import supported browser sets into a signed-in account. Deleting cloud app data does not erase browser-local data; you can clear that separately using your browser controls.

ACCOUNTS

Identity and sign-in

Account authentication is provided by Netlify Identity. Your email address is used to identify your account and support sign-in/password recovery. Authentication/session information is handled by the Identity service and the app uses the resulting signed-in session to access your account-scoped data.

The public Community view does not expose your account email or internal account ID. The in-app Delete My Cloud Data action deletes application data described below; it does not delete the separate Netlify Identity sign-in account.

CLOUD DATA

What signed-in users can save

  • Saved dice configurations.
  • Shortcut workspaces and shortcut options.
  • Custom Dice Studio sets.
  • Optional uploaded tray images attached to those dice sets.
  • Legacy theme records still retained for compatibility.
  • Community reports you submit and moderation records associated with your published sets.

These records are stored in account-scoped or app-scoped Netlify Blob storage. Production storage is separated from Deploy Preview/nonproduction storage.

COMMUNITY

What becomes public when you publish

A dice set can only be published after it is locked. Publishing creates a privacy-safe public projection of the set. The Community projection uses an opaque public ID, replaces the internal owner reference, and displays the creator as Adventurer.

Making a set private removes current public access. Republishing creates a fresh public identity. Public tray images are served through capability-scoped URLs and are configured not to use long-lived caching.

Publish intentionally. A public dice set's name, visual face labels/symbols, colors, and public tray image are meant to be seen by other users while the set is public.
RETENTION

How long application data remains

Server-stored application data is kept while you use the account so your saved configurations, shortcuts, Dice Studio sets, and related features remain available across sessions. The app does not apply a routine short expiration to those saved records.

When you use Delete My Cloud Data, the app removes its current server-stored records tied to your account, including saved configurations, shortcuts, Dice Studio records/images/public projections, legacy themes/images, authored Community reports, and moderation records tied to your owned sets. Public dice-set records are revoked fail-closed before child cleanup completes. If an administrator identity appears only as the actor on an unrelated moderation decision, that administrator identifier is anonymized instead of lifting the moderation decision.

Infrastructure providers may retain security, request, backup, or operational records under their own service policies and retention schedules. Those provider-managed records and the Netlify Identity sign-in account are outside the scope of the app's cloud-data deletion button.

EXPORT

Download a portable copy

Signed-in users can choose Export My Cloud Data from My Dice. The JSON export includes portable user-created settings and content such as configurations, shortcuts, dice-set customization, legacy theme settings, authored Community report text, and moderation status on owned sets.

For security, the export intentionally omits internal Blob storage keys, image capability tokens, and raw uploaded image bytes. It also does not include the separate Netlify Identity account or browser-local storage.

THIRD PARTIES

Services the app relies on

  • Netlify for hosting, serverless functions, Identity, and Blob storage.
  • DiceBox distribution CDNs (jsDelivr with unpkg fallback) for the standard 3D dice runtime/assets.
  • Buy Me a Coffee only when you choose the external Support link.

Those services may process technical request data according to their own policies when you use their service or follow an external link.

CONTROL

Your choices

  • You can use the core roller without an account.
  • You decide whether browser dice sets are imported into your account.
  • You decide whether a locked set is published to Community.
  • You can make an owned public set private again or delete an owned set from your collection.
  • You can download a JSON copy of server-stored app data from My Dice → Export My Cloud Data.
  • You can remove server-stored application data from My Dice → Delete My Cloud Data; the action requires typing a confirmation phrase.
  • You can clear local browser site data separately using your browser controls.

For a privacy request outside these in-app controls, contact the project owner through the repository's GitHub account.

SECURITY DESIGN

Protections built into the current app

Account writes require an authenticated session and trusted request origin. Cloud records are keyed by the authenticated account, stored with strong-consistency reads where appropriate, validated before use, and public dice-set reads re-check the authoritative owner record before exposure.

Uploaded cloud tray images must pass real PNG/JPEG/WebP pixel decoding and re-encoding before storage; metadata is stripped and unsafe or corrupt images fail closed. Account image storage is also bounded by per-image and aggregate limits.

No security design can guarantee that a hosted service will never experience an incident. Do not put private, sensitive, or identifying information into public dice-set names, face labels, or tray artwork.
