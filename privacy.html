PRIVACY

What the app stores and why.

This page describes the current production design in plain language. Last updated August 21, 2026.

GUEST MODE

Browser-local data

You can use the standard roller without creating an account. The app may store preferences and working data in your browser so the experience survives a reload. This can include sound/Keep Dice preferences, roll history, selected visual preferences, and browser-saved custom dice sets.

Browser-local data stays with that browser profile unless you explicitly import supported browser sets into a signed-in account.

ACCOUNTS

Identity and sign-in

Account authentication is provided by Netlify Identity. Your email address is used to identify your account and support sign-in/password recovery. Authentication/session information is handled by the Identity service and the app uses the resulting signed-in session to access your account-scoped data.

The public Community view does not expose your account email or internal account ID.

CLOUD DATA

What signed-in users can save

  • Saved dice configurations.
  • Shortcut workspaces and shortcut options.
  • Custom Dice & Tray Studio sets.
  • Optional uploaded tray images attached to those dice sets.

These records are stored in account-scoped Netlify Blob storage. Production storage is separated from Deploy Preview/nonproduction storage.

COMMUNITY

What becomes public when you publish

A dice set can only be published after it is locked. Publishing creates a privacy-safe public projection of the set. The Community projection uses an opaque public ID, replaces the internal owner reference, and displays the creator as Adventurer.

Making a set private removes current public access. Republishing creates a fresh public identity. Public tray images are served through capability-scoped URLs and are configured not to use long-lived caching.

Publish intentionally. A public dice set's name, visual face labels/symbols, colors, and public tray image are meant to be seen by other users while the set is public.

THIRD PARTIES

Services the app relies on

  • Netlify for hosting, serverless functions, Identity, and Blob storage.
  • DiceBox distribution CDNs (jsDelivr with unpkg fallback) for the standard 3D dice runtime/assets.
  • Buy Me a Coffee only when you choose the external Support link.

Those services may process technical request data according to their own policies when you use their service or follow an external link.

CONTROL

Your choices

  • You can use the core roller without an account.
  • You decide whether browser dice sets are imported into your account.
  • You decide whether a locked set is published to Community.
  • You can make an owned public set private again or delete an owned test/set from your collection.
  • You can clear local browser site data using your browser controls.

For an account/data request that is not available in the app UI, contact the project owner through the repository's GitHub account.

SECURITY DESIGN

Protections built into the current app

Account writes require an authenticated session and trusted request origin. Cloud records are keyed by the authenticated account, stored with strong-consistency reads where appropriate, validated before use, and public dice-set reads re-check the authoritative owner record before exposure.

No security design can guarantee that a hosted service will never experience an incident. Do not put private, sensitive, or identifying information into public dice-set names, face labels, or tray artwork.
