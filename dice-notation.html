DICE NOTATION

Dice notation explained: 1d20, 2d6+3, d100

Dice notation is a compact way to describe how many dice to roll and how many faces each die has. Once you can read XdY, most tabletop roll expressions become much easier to parse.

THE BASIC FORM

XdY means quantity × die size

In 2d6, the first number tells you to roll two dice and the second tells you that each die has six faces. 1d20 means one twenty-sided die. When the quantity is omitted, d8 normally means one d8.

MODIFIERS

A plus or minus is applied after the dice

2d6+3 means roll two d6, add their results, then add 3. Its raw dice range is 2–12; after the +3 modifier, the total range is 5–15. A minus modifier works the same way in the other direction.

PERCENTILE

d100 usually means a percentile result

Percentile dice commonly use two ten-sided dice: one represents tens and one represents ones. The familiar face sequence is 00, 10, 20, …, 90 on the tens die. Nothing But A Dice Roller preserves canonical percentile-style faces for its physical d100.
