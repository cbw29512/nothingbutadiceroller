FORMULA

What the calculator is computing

For a normal d20, the tool counts how many face results from 1–20 satisfy roll + modifier ≥ DC. Advantage succeeds unless both d20s fail. Disadvantage succeeds only when both d20s succeed. The calculator clamps impossible thresholds to 0% or 100% automatically.

EXAMPLE

Example: DC 15 with +5

A +5 modifier needs a raw roll of 10 or higher to meet DC 15. That is 11 successful faces out of 20, or 55% normally. The calculator then applies the two-d20 keep-higher / keep-lower model for advantage and disadvantage.

SCOPE

A transparent threshold model, not a rules override

This calculator models a simple d20 threshold: roll plus modifier must meet or beat the target. It does not assume game-specific automatic-success, automatic-failure, critical, or house-rule behavior.
