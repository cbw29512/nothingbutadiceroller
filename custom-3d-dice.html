DICE STUDIO SHOWCASE

Make custom 3D RPG dice without changing the roll

Dice Studio is the visual workshop behind Nothing But A Dice Roller. It exists for players who want digital dice to feel personal while keeping the standard die identity and numerical mechanics intact.

Open RollerDice ResourcesHow To
COLORS & GLOW

Build a coherent set or style one die

Choose body, face, and number-glow colors for the whole set, or separate a die when you want it to look different. Glow is visible in the editor and is represented in the generated physical texture without changing the numerical result.

CUSTOM FACES

Replace visible markings with symbols or short labels

Individual logical faces can display numbers, keyboard characters, symbols, runes, icons, or short words. A visual skull on face 20 remains logical result 20; the art does not rewrite the dice mathematics.

MATERIAL LOOK

Resin, finishes, patterns, and edge inlays

Dice Studio supports clear-resin style interiors, surface finishes, pattern treatments, and face-edge inlays. These controls are layered onto the visual theme so the familiar d4/d6/d8/d10/d12/d20/d100 identities remain recognizable.

SAVE & SHARE

Save sets and publish locked community copies

Guest-created sets can live in that browser. Signed-in users can keep account-backed sets, lock a completed design, and optionally publish a safe public projection to Community. Other users can use that public copy without receiving edit access to the owner's set.
