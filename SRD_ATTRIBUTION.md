# SRD Attribution

Nothing But A Dice Roller includes a limited set of structured, roll-relevant rules data derived from Wizards of the Coast's Dungeons & Dragons System Reference Documents.

- System Reference Document 5.1 (2014 rules corpus), Wizards of the Coast LLC.
- System Reference Document 5.2.1 (revised rules corpus), Wizards of the Coast LLC.

The SRD-derived material is used under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International license (CC BY 4.0):
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Official SRD information and downloads are available from D&D Beyond:
https://www.dndbeyond.com/srd

The repository's proprietary license applies to the project's original code, interface, artwork, product design, and other original material. It does not replace or restrict the CC BY 4.0 terms that apply to SRD-derived material.

The RAW shortcut catalog intentionally stores only the mechanical information needed to perform dice rolls. It does not reproduce full spell descriptions or other unnecessary SRD text.
