How to Use with TTRPGs (D&D / Pathfinder)

  • Ability Checks & Attacks: Tap + d20. Use Advantage or Disadvantage to automatically roll 2d20 and take the higher/lower value.
  • Damage & Spells: Tap your damage dice (e.g., d6, d8) to combine them into a single total.
  • Keep Dice Mode: Toggle Keep Dice ON if you want your selected dice pool to remain in the tray after rolling for rapid re-rolling.
  • Removing Dice: Tap any die in the tray to remove it individually.

No ads, no bloat—just a clean dice roller for your tabletop games.
