How to use shortcuts
  1. Choose examples: 2014 RAW and 2024 RAW each contain the same 3 cantrips plus 1 damaging spell per spell level. Pick the version your table uses.
  2. Keep RAW damage locked: these cards contain official spell damage only. They never include your character's attack bonus or other modifiers.
  3. Build your character's roll: make a separate Homebrew card with an icon and name, then add an optional attack roll, one or more damage groups, and any static damage modifier.
  4. Roll it: save your toolbar, return to the roller, press a shortcut, then press Roll Dice.

Use the RAW cards as examples when building your own combinations. You can remove every shortcut and keep using only the standard dice roller.
