SRD ATTRIBUTION

Dungeons & Dragons System Reference Documents

Nothing But A Dice Roller includes a limited set of structured, roll-relevant rules data derived from Wizards of the Coast's Dungeons & Dragons System Reference Documents:

  • System Reference Document 5.1 — 2014 rules corpus.
  • System Reference Document 5.2.1 — revised/2024 rules corpus.

The SRD-derived material is used under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International license (CC BY 4.0). Official SRD information and downloads are available from D&D Beyond.

The RAW shortcut catalog intentionally stores only the mechanical information needed to perform the supported dice rolls. It does not reproduce full spell descriptions or other unnecessary SRD text.

SERVICE NOTICE

Utility, not a rules authority

The app is designed to preserve the mechanics encoded in its supported RAW catalog and to keep Homebrew clearly separate. It is still a software utility, not an official rules service. If a tabletop rules dispute matters to your game, consult the applicable official rules source used by your table.
