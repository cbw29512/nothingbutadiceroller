Selected die look

This glows the printed numbers or symbols on every face of the selected die. Selecting face 4 keeps the result 4; it does not change the roll.

Clear resin & inside

This resin/interior look becomes the default for every die in the set.

Try clear resin with glitter, flakes, smoke, nebula, or bubbles. These are visual effects only; the physical die shape and result never change.

Surface finish

This surface finish becomes the default for every die in the set.

Matte, satin, gloss, metallic, and pearl finishes change only the generated surface artwork. Shape, physical result, and roll rules stay untouched.
